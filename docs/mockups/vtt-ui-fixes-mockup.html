Tabletop UI fixes

Three proposals from today's Company Mode test of the virtual tabletop, for your approval before anything is built. Gold dashed outlines mark what is new; everything else is the screen as it is today. Click the tokens and buttons: the mockup responds.

Correction to my last report. I said the map opens too zoomed in. That was wrong. The board opens fitted to the map picture. The test seed put the grid, and the hidden Little Brother, past the right edge of a small test picture, so he was off the board. The real gap is that nothing tells the GM a token is off the picture, and there is no list to reach it from. Proposal 1 now fixes that instead.

What changes

  • A list of every token on the live map, hidden ones in italics, with HP at a glance.
  • A Hide / Reveal button on each row, so revealing an enemy is one click and needs no map hunting.
  • A warning when a token sits off the map picture, with a button that brings it into view.
  • "Fit all" zooms the board out until every token and the whole picture fit.

Why: the tester could not find or reveal Little Brother at 1280, 1920 or 2560 pixels wide. Clicking a row is also the keyboard path to every token, which the board does not have today.

What changes

  • Click your own token, then click a hex. Dragging still works as it does today.
  • While your token is selected, the board shades where you can go: 1–4 spaces free (§5.5), 5–8 costing one Moment.
  • Keyboard: Tab to your token, arrow keys to pick a hex, Enter to move, Esc to cancel.
  • The shading is a guide, not a lock. The server still lets you place your token anywhere on the map, as now.

Why: drag is the only way to move today, which shuts out keyboard players and is fiddly on a phone.

What changes

  • The board takes the full width at the top, since it is what you look at most.
  • The side panels become four tabs under it: Body, Skills, Dice, Chat.
  • The Moment rail scrolls sideways inside its own strip, so Moments 6–10 are never cut off.
  • Above 600 px wide, the page stays exactly as it is now.

Why: at 375 px the page was wider than the screen. Moments 6–10 and the Torso and Right Arm chips were cut off.
