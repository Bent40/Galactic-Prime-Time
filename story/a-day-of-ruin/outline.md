## 🔒 THE REGISTRATION SEQUENCE (author, 2026-09-22)

Runs **during** beats 3–6. The System arrives mid-flight, not at a menu.

1. As he runs he thinks, and the system starts **downloading**.
2. Registration gate: **"Do you want to live?"**
3. He answers yes. It asks fields. **If he can't answer, it auto-detects.**
   Name · Current age · Sex · Sponsor.
4. Comedy by chaos: asked for **Sex**, an intrusive thought supplies *"last
   year"*; the system jabs back **"Not a date field."** Registration is a mess
   because the circumstances are.
5. **Sponsor** — he doesn't know. The system **scours his memories**, writes
   **Plutus**, and **burns the ticket into his thigh**: he left it in his pants.
6. Registration completes. As he is teleported: **"Welcome, sugar~!"** — Momus
   has his grasp on him.

### Why this works (recorded so it isn't re-litigated)

- **It pays the genre contract in the right place.** The LitRPG/progression
  reader promise is game-mechanic transparency; five chapters with no system
  reads to Royal Road as *not actually LitRPG*. Registration under chase
  pressure delivers it earlier and harder than the bible's Ch. 1 did.
- **It supersedes the Paul scene and is more canon-true than the original.**
  Canon: Plutus *tricked* Marcus, "consent not included" (owner, 2026-07-16).
  A ticket accepted in a pub is still a transaction he took part in. **A ticket
  forgotten in his laundry is not.** He never consented and never noticed.
- **"Do you want to live?" is the Forsaken trick as one line of UI** — he
  consents to the game believing he consents to survival. And it is a real
  question for a man the bible describes as *mediocrity*.
- **The brand has physical logic:** the object becomes the mark **where the
  pocket was.**

### Where Paul went

⚖ Proposed: Paul is **not cut** — he moves into the memory-scour as a retrieved
fragment (~80 words, mid-sprint), surfacing at the exact moment it explains
something. Canon keeps the blind man in the crumpled suit; the reader gets him
compressed and causal instead of as an opening scene. 🔴 Author's call.

### 🔴 Open calls this scene creates

1. **The auto-detect's limit.** A system that can read memories for a sponsor
   can read them for anything. ⚖ Proposed: auto-detect is **lazy and literal**
   — it grabs the nearest matching token, not the truth. It finds "Plutus"
   because the name is lying in a memory, not because it understands
   sponsorship. Keeps it funny, keeps it fallible, and makes it confidently
   wrong later.
2. **Thigh brand vs. the bible's chest brand** (*"a golden rectangle on the
   chest"*). ⚖ Proposed: keep **both** — the thigh scar is the *ticket*
   (private, shameful, what happened to him); the chest rectangle is champion
   *livery* (broadcast, applied separately, what he is to the audience).
3. **Does the sister get a field?** ⚖ If he doesn't think to ask about her
   until after he is gone, that is the arc's real gut-punch and it is free.
4. **Length discipline.** Registration is static comedy; the chase is kinetic.
   ⚖ Split the fields across two chapters (name/age in the streets,
   sex/sponsor in the chase, completion at the door) rather than one block —
   past roughly 600–700 words in one run it stops being *during* the chase and
   becomes *instead of* it.
5. **The patron tab.** The bible's HUD spec locks the patron icon. If the system
   writes Plutus in, brands him for it, and the tab is **still locked**, the
   Forsaken cruelty lands as a UI state for the price of one sentence. ⚖ Mine.

  his own. Deliver it there, or point at the withholding deliberately.
- 🔒 ⚖ **Age:** bible says 28; the photo says 26 and is established as out of
  date. Consistent if deliberate — confirm.

  game — never breathes between sentences, sign-off *"This is Momus. Stay
  tuned!"* The puppet already talks in that register, and *"Found you~!"* /
  *"Welcome, sugar~!"* tie the tildes together. Decide explicitly.

  prepare **hidden champions** ahead of time and the casino must *find* them —
  this is the Normal table going live, and Marcus is being collected out of it.
