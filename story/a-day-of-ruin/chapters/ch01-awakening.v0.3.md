A Day Of Ruin
Chapter one – Awakening
"Weekends, the result of the social consensus of being that rest is a necessity, nay, a basic human right!"
"Right."
"One would say it was given to us by the divine, right as the universe was created. Some would say it was decided upon by civilizations, once they became enlightened."
"Uh huh."
"In either case, the practice of resting on the weekend is something delightful, well documented, and well agreed upon."
"Get to the point, doofus. You're blocking the T.V."
"My point is, my beloved, wonderful sister, and I'll make it simple for you, since your brain's already mush, yeah? Why is your T.V. on full volume at 6 A.M. on a weekend, blasting through the house?" I said, pointing at the T.V. blasting some obscure version of Big Brother, only with a focus on getting convicted felons together for love.
The things these people come up with, I swear.
My sister was sitting at the edge of her bed, her feet dangling from the end of it, her eyes staring at me with the attention span of a goldfish. Clearly whatever was on the T.V. was more important to her than her dear, suffering brother.
"Because," she said, pushing me aside with her leg, shoving a handful of popcorn into her mouth "It’s the season finale. We're finally gonna find out if the last couple can pull through, especially after last episode."
"This is fucking stupid. Put the volume down or I'm putting you down." My finger moved from the T.V. to her and stayed there for a few seconds before I headed to leave her room. As I went out and reached to close her door, I realized one of the actors in this circus we call a house is missing. "By the way, where's Mom?"
"Left for a vacation to the Philippines with her friends, remember? She told us a few weeks ago that was her plan. Should be landing in a couple of hours." She played around with her popcorn, shoving another handful without even looking in my direction.
I closed her door and headed back to my room, which is right around the corner from hers. We shared a wall, onto which she hung up the T.V. which, unfortunately for me, means I hear her T.V. like it's just above me. The little psycho makes sure to follow the latest trendy shows, which means I involuntarily do too.
I got back into bed, and fell asleep for what I hoped would be the next 24 hours, or realistically, until 10 A.M. We don’t always get what we want, however.
I woke up to a loud unidentified sound outside my window. Sounds like something slashing through the air, cutting my sleep abruptly. I bet she's watching a war movie or something. If it's war she wants, war she will get.
I got out of bed and headed to her room. That T.V. is going down, and my sister might be next. I opened the door to her room and there she was, just as expected, hiding beneath her bed, watching the news.
"What the fuck is wrong with you? Can't you at least keep it down?" I look at her screen as the newscaster prattles on.
"…The assailants are creatures of fantasy, weird gremlins, wild animals, warriors in furs and paint, with some reporting seeing a dragon. It is a mess out there. Stay in your homes, close your windows, don’t go out and don’t make a sound. The army is successfully repelling the forces…"
I look back at my sister. "Decent setting, honestly. Sounds like a good movie. Send me the name later. And keep it down!" I took a minute to look at her. She was horrified. Like, pissing her pants horrified. On the T.V., the caster's neck twists in those horribly grotesque, CGI ways, and his skin glows bright yellow. Pretty neat, honestly. After a few sound effects that were closer to crackers breaking than anything human bones should be making, followed by gasps of horror from what I assume to be the filming staff, the newscaster looks back directly at the camera, with a smile full of teeth that are clearly fake.
"This is really high budget, holy hell."
"We interrupt your breaking news to break some news for ya! Welcome, Fleshlings, to our brand-new season of [Insert show name that isn’t galactic prime time]! The rules are simple! We give tasks to our lovely contestants and bet on how they fare! Our contestants being you of course! Yes, you! the ones watching us!"
The caster claps with seemingly fake joy.
"Now, ladies and gentlemen, we're almost ready to start the first episode. Just one, teeny tiny issue. We're unable to locate some of our actors! We'd love it if you could help us find them!" On the screen appear 2 photos.
The first photo depicts a man of European descent, seemingly in his early 20s. He has a mohawk and his clothes are all black. The shirt he had on seems to reference some show with the words "Blood & guts" written in dark red. The name Daniel Krieg was written below. 
The second photo was much more familiar, however. It looked like an American youth, 26 in age, scruffy, short hair. He was wearing gray sweatpants and a stained plain shirt, the result of a food-related accident, no doubt. On his feet were both flip flops and socks, and his glasses fell crooked on his nose, which was odd, considering I haven’t worn my glasses for the past few months now.
That guy was me.
Oh fuck. This isn’t a movie, is it?
"Anyone who turns them in might get a special prize~! Come on dearies, you want to survive, don't you? BRING. THEM. TO. US. Plain and simple, just like you guys!"
Fuck me, they are hunting me and this… Daniel dude down. That’s creepy.
"What the fuck did you do? Why are you on the news?!"
Ah, now she can speak.
"How the fuck should I know how Twists McGee got my name on his fucking list?" Clearly their photo is outdated as well. Maybe it's off a random list they got. "Who are they anyway?"
The photo on the T.V. changed again. It now shows my current clothing. As well as the look on my face. Terrified, that is.
"How did that – " My sister did not finish that sentence, because I had already pulled her away from the flying dragon thing in the window.
The image on the T.V. was warping to all hell, and the last thing I could make out before I got out of the room was a slimy "Found you~!"
