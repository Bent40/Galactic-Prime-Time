**Floor 3 is the grand capital, a hundred years after Floor 2** — and **170 after
Floor 1**. It is the Set 1 finale, and afterwards the capital attaches to the Lounge
as a persistent location (§4.1).

### All three routes converge on the plague — and there are TWO cures

This is the floor's spine, and it is already in canon:

| Route | What it wants cured |
|---|---|
| **Easy** | Nullrot *is* the plague and the cure, in one body |
| **Medium** | a demon wants a **human sacrifice** to cure **his demonic nature** |
| **Hard** | demons hunt the Loong because its cure might work on **their nature** |

**So the capital holds a cure for the crystal and a hoped-for cure for being a demon,
and nobody has established whether they are the same thing.** They are. Nothing in the
city knows it, because nothing in the city shares anything — which is the floor's
theme rendered as a fact rather than a speech.

### ⚠️ The timeline forces a design problem, and the F1 ruling already solved it

**F3 is 170 years after F1**, so **Foreman Bex should be dead** — and §4.4 says he is
running the human farm. The Beelzebub chain from F1 supplies the answer: *he was
kept*, because the farm is useful. That is now his encounter (C-2) rather than a
continuity hole.

## Layer A — The Grand Capital (shared)

| # | Entry | Size | HP | The gate, in one line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A-1** | **Quarantine Enforcer** | Medium | 5 | none — **and they are probably right** |
| **A-2** | **Crystal Bloom** | Large | 5 | **damaging it spreads it**; only Burn destroys it cleanly |
| **A-3** | **Clay Servitor** | Large | 5 | repairs from any brick — **deface the inscription and it drops** |
| **A-4** | **Bellringer** | Medium | 5 | **if it rings, the street answers** — 2 mobs a Clock, forever |
| **A-5** | **Gilt Rat** | Small | 5 | trivial to kill; **taking what it carries is the trap** |
| **A-6** | **Mirror-Bronze Warden** | Large | **75** | **it reflects** every ranged attack back at you |
| **A-7** | **Silver-Limbed Surgeon** | Medium | **44** | **it will not let you die** — and takes the part it judges lost |

**A-1 is the floor's opening argument.** The Enforcers have held the crystal back for
a century by burning districts. The party is walking through a quarantine whose terms
they never read, and every one of these is a person doing a job **that works**.

**A-2 and A-4 make the capital a stealth-and-tools floor.** A party that hacks its way
down a street makes the street worse; a party that fights loudly summons the street.
After two floors of open ground, that is a real change of register.

**A-6 punishes the ranged opening** the desert taught them — anything thrown, fired,
coned or cast comes straight back. **Dull the Polished Face (14)** with acid, soot,
mud, blood or Resin, and one Moment of thinking beats a Clock of shooting.
**Carve:** Mirror-Bronze.

**A-7 is the one thing on this floor that can be talked to.** It is not hostile, it is
*unsupervised*. Killing it is easy, cheap, and loses the party the best surgeon in the
capital — right before a floor that will take limbs off them. **Carve:** Silver, the
prosthetic metal.

## Layer B — Easy Route: Nullrot

Beats (§4.3): the man from the staircase is now **Nullrot**, simultaneously spreading
and curing a disease in the capital. **Fight, or help.**

| # | Entry | HP | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B-1** | **Nullrot's Faithful** | 5 | they interpose, unarmed, and **will not stop** |
| **B-2** | **The First Cured** | **66** | the proof the cure works — **killing it destroys the evidence** |
| **B-3** | ☠☠ **Nullrot** | **300** | the Set 1 finale |

**B-1 also returns F1's Crystallized Citizens as a tide of ~50** in the quarantined
districts — still Crush-only, still people, and now there are fifty.

**B-2 is the witness.** The first person he saved, 170 years ago, still alive and still
crystal in places — which proves the cure **arrests** the plague rather than removing
it. It argues rather than fights, and it is the only thing that can explain what
Nullrot actually *is* before the party meets him.

### B-3 · ☠☠ Nullrot — SUPER BOSS (300)

**Parts:** **Mask 30** · Head 26 · Torso 70 · Arms 24/24 · Legs 26/26 ·
**The Reservoir 40** · **Halo of Cures 34** · **Large**

**He is both patient zero and the antibody, and that is a body rather than a paradox:**
the **Reservoir** holds the plague, the **Halo** is what he sheds walking down a
street. He cannot have one without the other. Neither can anyone else.

> **The party did this.** The Doorward at F2 had been eating the plague out of him for
> seventy years. They killed it to free him — **correctly, on the evidence they had** —
> and the Reservoir has been filling ever since. Nobody needs to tell them. Someone will.

**The win condition is the argument**, and the argument is genuinely hard, because
**he is right**: killing him ends the cure, and the capital has a century of people
who are only alive because of the Halo.

**Destroying the Reservoir (40) alone is the surgical answer.** It is deep, guarded,
and takes the Mask down first. It ends the plague and leaves the cure. It also, almost
certainly, ends him — and the party will not be told that until afterwards.

**Phases:** *The Round* (he doesn't stop working; **the Halo cures the party while they
decide whether to kill him**) → *The Reservoir Answers* (he doesn't retaliate — the
Reservoir does) → *Both At Once* (**the phase where the argument is winnable**, because
the table can finally *see* what he's been saying) → *The Mask Decides* (a TPK from
here is expected; if the Mask falls, the man beneath is 170 years old and dies of it
within a Clock, lucid, and grateful).

## Layer C — Medium Route: the human farm

Beats (§4.4): the surviving NPC leader runs a **human farm** in the capital; a demon
wants a **specific human sacrifice** to cure his demonic nature. **Sacrifice one, or
kill him.**

| # | Entry | HP | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C-1** | **Farmhand** | 5 | they fight to keep their jobs, and surrender readily |
| **C-2** | **Foreman Bex, Kept** | **85** | 170 years old, and **he should not be** |
| **C-3** | ☠ **The Petitioner** | **150** | he has the paperwork |

**C-2 — he cannot be killed while he is owned.** Parts return to 1 HP each Clock,
because something is paying for that. **The Ledger (5)** is the same ledger, still
being kept, and it names his patron on the last page. Destroy it — or **read it aloud
where the crowd can hear** (§17) — and the arrangement is void: he ages in front of
them. **He does not defend it. He has been waiting a long time for someone to take it.**

*(Filed under a distinct name from his F1 entry because the seeder matches by name.)*

### C-3 · ☠ The Petitioner — boss (150) ⚖

**The specific human is in Bex's pens, has a name, and the party meets them first.**
That ordering *is* the encounter — it is not a trolley problem until you've shaken the
trolley's hand.

**Gate — the Petition (14):** a formal claim on his own nature. While it's intact he
cannot be killed by anything that is not a refusal, and Torso damage is cosmetic.

**Three ways out, and they are the whole scene:**
- **Grant it.** He becomes mortal, keeps his word, and the party carries it. Enormous
  Exposure. The crowd loves it and nobody else will.
- **Refuse it.** Destroy the Petition; he is killable and a demon forever — **which he
  already knew when he asked.**
- **Answer it another way.** Nullrot's cure or the Loong's may work on a demon. A
  Medium party can't reach either — **but they can know, and knowing is a bargaining
  chip.**

## Layer D — Hard Route: the Loong in hiding

Beats (§4.4): the Loong hides in the capital **preventing disease spread**; demons hunt
it as a cure for their nature; the party helps it develop the cure and protects it.

| # | Entry | HP | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D-1** | **Ashen Inquisitor** | 5 | killing one is fine — **letting one leave is not** |
| **D-2** | **The Cartographer** | **51** | **a timer wearing a person** |
| **D-3** | ☠ **The One Who Would Be Human** | **170** | it wants what Nullrot gives away free |

**D-2 doesn't fight and doesn't need to.** Every Clock it survives, one more district
is marked clear. Four Clocks and it finds the Loong regardless of what the party does.
**Burn the map, not the demon** — killing the Cartographer and leaving the map just
means the map is inherited.

### D-3 · ☠ The One Who Would Be Human — boss (170) ⚖

**It is not a villain and the party should struggle to treat it as one.** It wants what
the Petitioner wants and what Nullrot gives away free. The difference is that it will
take the Loong apart to get it — **and the Loong is a friend now.**

**Gate — The Wanting (14):** it cannot be reasoned with, driven off or repositioned.
Every Clock it moves one space closer to the Loong regardless of what is in the way.

**Weak system — give it what it wants.** The Loong's cure works on demons, and the
Loong will say so if asked. Offering it ends the fight outright and permanently, and
costs nothing but the thing they came to protect being *used*. Destroying the Wanting
is the other answer, and it leaves a corpse that died reaching for a cure.

**The third answer, if they worked it out: there is enough for both.**

> ### The Loong is why the capital is still standing
> Same 300-point block. **It has held the plague back for a century by being here** —
> so **it cannot fight without leaving, and leaving means the disease moves.**
>
> That is F2's escort problem inverted: last floor the party moved it to safety, this
> floor they must keep it **still** while everything comes to it.
