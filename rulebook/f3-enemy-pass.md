## F3-0 — The design rule for this floor ✅ **RULED 2026-08-18**

> **Every mob is a CONSEQUENCE of a previous floor, not a thing that happens to be here.**

The capital is where the party **finds the climax of the story they have been in since
Floor 1**, and they may perform it however they like. **This is not a stealth floor** —
it is not a floor "about" any tactic. Loud, quiet, legal, criminal, diplomatic or
arson: the shape of the ending is theirs.

So the roster is people and consequences, not set-pieces: **infected citizens, rogue
demons, hoodlums, crystal clusters**, and — by route — **royal guards**. Each one
answers a question the party created.

|---|---|
| **Band** | M-3 Capital **×8** — Jade · Mirror-Bronze · Silver · Inscribed Clay · Orichalcum · Cursed Gold ⭐ |
| **Party** | level ~36, 44 trait points, **11 HP torso** |
| **Enemy HP** | unchanged — mob 5 · elite ~60 · boss ~125 · **super ~300** |

| **Returning** | F1 mobs as tides of **~50**, F2 mobs as tides of **~25** |

### All three routes converge on the plague — and there are two cures

**There are TWO cures, they are DIFFERENT, and each is held by whoever does not need it.**

| Cure | Held by | Wanted by |
|---|---|---|
| **The plague cure** | **Nullrot** — he is patient zero *and* the antibody | **the Loong**, which has been holding the plague back by standing in the city |
| **Loong blood** — cures demonic tendencies | **the Loong** | **every demon on the floor** — Bex, the One Who Would Be Human, the hunt |

**Neither party knows the other exists.** Nullrot could free the Loong; the Loong's
blood could end Bex's petition without a sacrifice and stop the hunts. Nobody in this
city has put it together, because nothing in this city shares anything — the floor's
theme as a fact rather than a speech.

### The path of least casualties ✅ **owner, 2026-08-18**

**Get the Loong out of the city · make the Loong and Nullrot meet in the capital ·
help the queen revive her god.**

- Nullrot's cure takes over containment, so **the Loong no longer has to stand still**.
- Loong blood then cures demonic nature freely — **Bex needs no human sacrifice**, and
  the hunts have nothing left to hunt.
- A revived god gives the demons a patron and direction again, so **the rogue demons
  stop being rogue**.

🔴 **This requires knowledge from all three routes**, and §4.1 says a party runs *one*
route per campaign. Whether the capital is where the routes converge — or whether the
optimal ending is deliberately out of any single party's reach — is an open call.

### The quarantine is a place, not an obstacle

Half the floor is behind the line. The people inside are still alive. **Getting in is
easy; getting back out is the ask.** The Enforcers have held the crystal off for a
century this way, and they are, on the evidence, right.

| **A-4** | **Hoodlum** | Medium | 5 | none — but **they know the walls** | the quarantine itself |
| **A-5** | **Crystal Cluster** | Large | 5 | **damaging it spreads it**; only Burn is clean | the plague, and the party's route |
| **A-6** | **Mirror-Bronze Warden** | Large | **75** | **it reflects** every ranged attack | the capital being *old* |
| **A-7** | **Silver-Limbed Surgeon** | Medium | **44** | **it will not let you die** | a century of A-2 |

**A-2 is the floor's real weight.** Not crystal yet — coughing, frightened, and
entirely themselves. **Every one the party kills was a person they could have kept**,
curable with Antiseptic Wash, Burn T2, or twenty minutes near Nullrot's Halo. §17 is
watching them decide which.

**A-3 branches directly on Floor 1.** Girl killed → these are the demons who never bent
the knee to Beelzebub: **everywhere, in numbers, and the city has no idea why it got
worse.** Girl spared → far fewer, still under orders, and therefore **talkable**.
Same statline; the count and the conversation change. The party made this.

**A-6 punishes the ranged habit** the desert taught. Dull the **Polished Face (14)** —
acid, soot, mud, blood, Resin — and one Moment of thinking beats a Clock of shooting.
It is the one thing here that predates the party's whole story.

**A-7 is the only thing on this floor that can be talked to.** Not hostile —
*unsupervised*. Killing it is easy, cheap, and loses the capital's best surgeon right
before a floor that will take limbs off them.

## Layer B — Easy Route: Nullrot

Beats (§4.2): the man from the staircase is now **Nullrot**, spreading and curing a
disease in the capital. **Fight, or help.**

| # | Entry | HP | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B-1** | **Nullrot's Faithful** | 5 | they interpose, unarmed, and **will not stop** |
| **B-2** | **The First Cured** | **66** | the proof the cure works — **killing it destroys the evidence** |
| **B-3** | ☠☠ **Nullrot** | **300** | the Set 1 finale |

### B-3 · ☠☠ Nullrot — SUPER BOSS (300)

**Parts:** **Mask 30** · Head 26 · Torso 70 · Arms 24/24 · Legs 26/26 ·
**The Reservoir 40** · **Halo of Cures 34** · **Large**

**Both patient zero and the antibody — a body, not a paradox.** The **Reservoir** holds
the plague; the **Halo** is what he sheds walking down a street. He cannot have one
without the other. Neither can anyone else.

> **The party did this.** The F2 Doorward had been eating the plague out of him for
> seventy years. They killed it to free him — **correctly, on the evidence they had**.

**The win condition is the argument**, and it is hard because **he is right**: killing
him ends the cure, and the capital has a century of people alive only because of the
Halo. **Destroying the Reservoir (40) alone** ends the plague and leaves the cure — and
almost certainly ends him, which nobody mentions until afterwards.

**Phases:** *The Round* (**the Halo cures the party while they decide whether to kill
him**) → *The Reservoir Answers* (he doesn't retaliate — it does) → *Both At Once*
(**where the argument becomes winnable**, because the table can finally see it) →
*The Mask Decides* (TPK expected; if the Mask falls, the man beneath is 170 and dies of
it within a Clock, lucid and grateful).

## Layer C — Medium Route: the human farm

Beats (§4.3), read as **one person**: the surviving NPC leader runs a human farm, and
**he — being a demon —** wants a specific human sacrifice to cure his demonic nature.

| # | Entry | HP | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C-1** | **Royal Guard** | 5 | the farm is **licensed**, and they have read the paperwork |
| **C-2** | **The Bought Magistrate** | **58** | he doesn't fight, **he calls** |
| **C-3** | ☠ **Bex, the Petitioner** | **155** | the man from the burning house |

**C-1 branches on the Girl too, one layer up.** Killed → the crown is human, frightened,
and licensed the farm because Beelzebub's people asked. Spared → **the crown is hers**,
the guards know what they serve, and the farm is a concession to a tolerated noble.

**C-2's weak system is silence, not death.** Every Clock he can speak, a lawful squad
arrives and *the party* are the criminals. Grapple him, Chill his head, cover his
mouth, walk him out of earshot. **Killing him is easiest and worst:** a dead magistrate
makes the farm a crime scene the crown must defend, and **the pens are emptied that
night**, in the way that phrase implies.

### C-3 · ☠ Bex, the Petitioner — boss (155)

> ### Bex was always the rival demon
>
> **Floor 1** — he leads humans to burn a house with a demon child inside. The party
> assumed plot armour. **He was a demon killing a rival by proxy.**
> **Floor 2** — revealed as the Rival Noble, *"the demon who helped humans and wants to
> overthrow her"*. They were sent to assassinate him. **They may have succeeded. It
> does not take** — a demon is not killed by killing its body, and nobody told them.
> **Floor 3** — the farm. He has been breeding and buying humans for a hundred and
> seventy years looking for **one**.

**He has found them, and the party meets that person, by name, before they meet him.**
That ordering *is* the encounter — it is not a trolley problem until you've shaken the
trolley's hand.

**He wants to be human.** He has spent his existence pretending to be one, badly, and
manipulating them because it was the only way he knew to be among them. Everything he
did across three floors was this. **He is not lying and never has been** — the Petition
does not permit it.

**Gate — The Petition (14).** A formal claim on his own nature. While intact he cannot
be killed by anything that is not a refusal; Torso damage is cosmetic.

**Three ways out:**
- **Grant it.** Hand him the human. He becomes mortal and keeps his word, and the party
  carries it. Enormous Exposure. The crowd loves it and nobody else will.
- **Refuse it.** Destroy the Petition; he is killable — properly, this time — and a
  demon forever, **which he knew when he asked.**
- **Answer it another way.** Nullrot's cure and the Loong's work on demons. A Medium
  party can't reach either — **but they can know, and knowing is a bargaining chip.**

**Phases:** *The Foreman* (he receives them as the man from Floor 1, same manner, same
apologies — **if they never worked it out, the voice does it for them**) → *The Asking*
→ *The Pressing* (170 years of patience ending in about four Moments) → *Denied*.

## Layer D — Hard Route: the Loong in hiding

| # | Entry | HP | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D-1** | **Ashen Inquisitor** | 5 | killing one is fine — **letting one leave is not** |
| **D-2** | **The Cartographer** | **51** | **a timer wearing a person** |
| **D-3** | ☠ **The One Who Would Be Human** | **170** | it wants what Nullrot gives away free |

**D-2 doesn't fight and doesn't need to.** Four Clocks of survival and it finds the
Loong regardless. **Burn the map, not the demon** — kill it and leave the map, and the
map is inherited.

**D-3 is not a villain and the party should struggle to treat it as one.** It wants
what Bex wants and what Nullrot gives away free. The difference is it will take the
Loong apart to get it. **The Wanting (14)** cannot be reasoned with or repositioned —
every Clock it moves one space closer, and the party is fighting a compass.

**Weak system — give it what it wants.** The cure works on demons and the Loong will
say so if asked. **The third answer, if they worked it out: the Loong's blood is not scarce, and Nullrot can free it to give some.**

> ### The Loong is why the capital is still standing
> It has held the plague off for a century **by being here** — so it cannot fight
> without leaving, and leaving means the disease moves. **F2's escort, inverted:**
> last floor they moved it to safety; this floor they keep it **still** while
> everything comes to it.
