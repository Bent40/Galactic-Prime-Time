# Set 1 — the nine item concepts (PROPOSAL, 2026-08-25)

**The first 9 of the ~27 authored by W-9 R-1.** Set 1 is F1–F3, three routes × three
acquisition classes. **There is no floor axis here** — §12.7's material band carries each
concept from F1 to F3 on its own (M-1 ×2 → M-2 ×4 → M-3 ×8), so every concept below is written
**once** and read three times.

> **Graded against the corpus in [`weapon-research.md`](weapon-research.md).** Every design
> call below cites the finding it answers. Where the corpus and the rulings disagree, **the
> rulings win** — W-9 R-2 rejects the arbitrary consent gates the sources actually used.

**This is the SPINE, not the top shelf.** `items-batch-c.js` already carries the authored apex
pieces (Chains of the Stairway Prophet · Nullrot's Bell · The Prophet's Toll · Queensfang ·
Loong Blood Phial · Loong-Scale Aegis). W-7 §1 puts the genre's apex ratio near **3%**, so those
stay few. These nine are the backbone every route should reliably offer.

🔴 **Nothing here is ruled.** Nine concepts, no stat blocks — numbers come after the shapes are
blessed. Open calls are in **C-10**.

## C-0 — The grid

| | 🎯 **loot** (it drops) | **crafted** (from a carve) | **story** (only after a problem is solved) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Easy** — the plague · the mask · Nullrot | **Leak-Vial** — unsorted seepage; disease or cure, and you cannot tell yet | **Oathbreaker line** — cuts what is warded, because you are owed the cut | **The Name** — makes a monster answerable instead of killable |
| **Medium** — demon politics · Bex · Vermilia | **Clan-Token** — proof taken off a demon whose death did not take | **Brand-Iron** — carry a mark that grants immunity, publicly | **The Debt** — a girl's thanks, which may become a queen's writ |
| **Hard** — the Loong · the hunt · the crystal | **Crystal Shard** — the plague as a hazard you choose to carry | **Kin-Carve** — and overuse makes you kin | **The Horn, taken** — you hold the thing that calls the hunt |

**Grading rule applied throughout (W-7 §2):** the **loot** column is graded against loot games
(56% ungated boss drops), the **crafted** and **story** columns against myth (which gates on
requirement 64%, cost 40%). That is why the loot row carries the fewest predicates and the
story row carries the heaviest.

## C-1 — Easy × loot · **Leak-Vial**

|||
|---|---|
| **essence** | restoration / world-scale — *unsorted* |
| **quant/cat** | **categorical** — it is a coin-flip between two categorical effects, never a number |
| **requirement_shape** | 🎯 **none.** Loot, and W-7 §2 grades loot against games, which leave 56% of apex drops ungated |
| **cost** | 🔴 **you do not know which one you are holding** |

The mask leaks. Nullrot's Reservoir holds the plague and his Halo sheds the cure, and what
seeps out of a sealed door is **both, unseparated**. A vial of it is the Easy route's most
honest object: the campaign's central fact — *patient zero and the antibody are the same
creature* — as a thing in your bag.

**Band readings**

| | Material | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** ×2 | Resin (M-1) | Sealed in forest resin. **Unsorted** — using it is a gamble the table takes knowingly |
| **F2** ×4 | Sunglass (M-2) | Seventy years of Doorward feeding has thinned it. Still unsorted, but the tell exists if anyone thinks to look |
| **F3** ×8 | Mirror-Bronze (M-3) | Having met Nullrot, **the party can sort it.** Same object, and knowledge is what turned a gamble into a tool |

⚙️ **Why this cell:** the loot column needs volume and needs to be ungated. The *appreciation*
here is by **knowledge**, not deeds — a deliberate sibling to R-3 rather than an instance of it,
kept separate so R-3's forward-accrual stays clean.

## C-2 — Easy × crafted · **the Oathbreaker line** ⭐

|||
|---|---|
| **essence** | 🔴 **bypass** — the highest-value categorical power in GPT's math (W-6 §3) |
| **quant/cat** | **categorical** |
| **requirement_shape** | 🔒 **`REQUIRES Vengeful` · `REFUSES Corporate Asset`** |
| **cost** | none at use — the requirement *is* the price (W-6 §1: myth gates on requirement 64%, cost 40%) |

**This concept already exists and this is a restatement, not an invention.** Mistletoe's seed
carries `Charm 8` with the note: *"Oathbreaker is a **CLAIM**, not an edge. It cuts what is
warded because **you are owed the cut**, and a contestant nobody would side with is holding a
sprig."* `set1-story-canon.md` records that the Mask's Oathbreaker gate *"stops being
arbitrary."*

🔴 **That Charm 8 is a predicate wearing a stat's clothes.** R-2 lets it be written directly:

- **`REQUIRES Vengeful`** — *"Take a significant action specifically to repay harm done to you
  or someone you care about."* **That is the claim, verbatim.** You are owed the cut.
- **`REFUSES Corporate Asset`** — *"Complete a Directive without deviation, complaint, or asking
  what it's for."* 🔴 **Someone executing an order has no claim of their own to make.** The two
  are structurally incompatible, which is exactly the NOT-BE shape R-2 asks for.

⚠️ **Recommendation: keep Charm 8 as well, or drop it?** Flagged in C-9 — the predicate is the
truer gate, but removing a shipped stat requirement touches seeded data.

**Band readings**

| | Material | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** ×2 | **Mistletoe** ⭐ (M-1) | The sprig. Bypasses ward/immunity — the answer to THE MASKED's Oathbreaker weak system |
| **F2** ×4 | Turquoise ⭐ (M-2) | The desert's version of *the thing that was not sworn* — the sun's dart, owed to nobody |
| **F3** ×8 | **Cursed Gold** ⭐ (M-3) | Andvari's hoard: *it wants things.* A claim-weapon made of the metal that makes claims **is the concept eating itself**, and that is the right note to end Set 1 on |

## C-3 — Easy × story · **The Name**

|||
|---|---|
| **essence** | **reciprocity** — it reads the target's stance and answers it (the Narayanastra shape, W-4b) |
| **quant/cat** | **categorical** |
| **requirement_shape** | 🔒 **`REFUSES Butcher`** |
| **cost** | it does not work twice on the same creature |

The staircase man is still in there, and at F3 he *"can be talked to and answers honestly,
which is worse than a monster."* Canon says **only RECOGNITION was ever missing.** The Name is
that, as an object: it does not damage, it makes a thing **answerable** — forcing the scene
where there would have been a fight.

🔒 **`REFUSES Butcher`** — *"Reduce an enemy to component parts. Deliberately."* A contestant
who does that **cannot hold the thing that asks a monster its name.** Decidable, on the sheet,
and it makes the party choose what kind of act they are known for.

**Band readings**

| | Material | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** ×2 | Oak Heartwood (M-1) | A mural-fragment, unreadable. **It does nothing yet** — the story has not happened |
| **F2** ×4 | Flint (M-2) | The Doorward *warns them aloud and truthfully*. First time the object answers back |
| **F3** ×8 | **Inscribed Clay** (M-3) | *The writing is the strength.* The host's name, and the argument F3 is actually about |

⚙️ **Why this cell:** myth's story class is 44% of its acquisitions (W-6 §5) and is gated on
**conduct** (the astra system: transmission *"only after the student's character had been
established"*; Solo Leveling's System choosing Jinwoo for staying behind to die). C-3 is that
rule pointed at the Easy route's actual question.

## C-4 — Medium × loot · **Clan-Token**

|||
|---|---|
| **essence** | **dominion** — social, not damaging |
| **quant/cat** | **categorical** |
| **requirement_shape** | 🔒 **`REQUIRES Menace`** |
| **cost** | it names you to that clan — in both directions |

🔒 The clan law: *a demon dies only when its CLAN dies.* So a body-part taken off a demon whose
death **did not take** is not a trophy, it is a **clan artifact** — and demons of that clan must
acknowledge it. On a route that is entirely demon politics, a social bypass is worth more than
a weapon.

🔒 **`REQUIRES Menace`** — *"Cause an enemy to retreat, surrender, or hesitate purely based on
your presence or reputation."* You cannot present a clan's own token and be ignorable.

**Band readings**

| | Material | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** ×2 | Beastbone (M-1) | Off one of Bex's arsonists. Nobody knows what it is, including the party |
| **F2** ×4 | Sky-Iron (M-2) | Bex's assassination *does not take* — and this is the proof left behind |
| **F3** ×8 | Jade (M-3) | The high court are other-clan demons. **A token they recognise is a door into a palace that does not know its own secret** |

## C-5 — Medium × crafted · **Brand-Iron**

|||
|---|---|
| **essence** | **oath/geas** — binds, and charges the wielder |
| **quant/cat** | **categorical** |
| **requirement_shape** | 🔒 **`REFUSES Spy`** |
| **cost** | 🔴 **the mark is public, and readable by anyone who knows the script** |

Vermilia grants **the brand** at F1, and at F2 it is full immunity to the Dissolution songs.
Brand-Iron is the brand made portable: you can put the mark on someone else. Immunity, in
exchange for **wearing whose you are** where the cameras can see it.

🔒 **`REFUSES Spy`** — *"Operate without anyone — ally or enemy — knowing your real objective
for an entire session."* 🔴 **A mark that announces your allegiance and a contestant whose
objective is unknown cannot coexist.** The cleanest NOT-BE in the set: it is not a judgment on
the player, it is a structural contradiction.

⭐ **Name the smith (W-8 §6).** Every crafter-gated source in the corpus names one maker —
Ashul Edwaru's 13, Dubs's six Sacred Treasures, the Norse dwarves. GPT's carve currently names
none. **Brand-Iron should be made by a specific high-court demon who does not know what the
crown's secret is** — which makes the tranche-2 finding usable: *coerced craft comes out
flawed.* Commission it politely and it is clean; lean on them and it carries a defect.

**Band readings**

| | Material | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** ×2 | Tough Hide (M-1) | The brand as given. One bearer, no transfer |
| **F2** ×4 | Scorpion Chitin (M-2) | Light — *−1 Physique requirement.* Now it can be carried and applied to an ally mid-route |
| **F3** ×8 | **Silver** (M-3) | Nuada's arm, the prosthetic metal. The mark becomes **part of the body**, and stops being removable |

## C-6 — Medium × story · **The Debt** ⭐ the R-3 exemplar

|||
|---|---|
| **essence** | 🔒 **investiture** — possession *is* the claim (W-4b: Mjölnir · Kusanagi · Zulfiqar) |
| **quant/cat** | **categorical** |
| **requirement_shape** | 🔒 **`REFUSES Witnessed`** |
| **cost** | none — 🔴 **and that is the point.** Its value is entirely what the party did |

**This is W-9 R-3 demonstrated in one object.** The party frees a girl from a burning house.
She gives them something small — a token of thanks, worth nothing, no effect. Then:

- **Spared branch:** F2 she is a queen. F3 she is *the entire dynasty*. **The same worthless
  token is now a writ from the crown of the world.** The object never changed. What was done
  with it did.
- 🔴 **Killed branch:** it is a keepsake off a corpse and **stays inert forever** — a permanent,
  silent record of the campaign's heaviest consequence, sitting in the party's inventory.

⭐ *"Weapons can birth myths"* (R-3): this one births the myth **and then the myth outranks the
weapon.** It closes W-6 §3's gap — *GPT has no way for an item's MEANING to appreciate* — with
no new subsystem, because appreciation here is just the story having happened.

🔒 **`REFUSES Witnessed`** — *"Observe a significant event without intervening. The crowd
respects this. Or resents it."* 🔴 **Vermilia's entire sin is that she watched and did not
speak.** Her question is *"was I a coward?"* An object of her gratitude that **refuses a
contestant who stood by** is her own unanswerable judgment, turned outward and made mechanical.
A party member with `Witnessed` cannot carry it — and the reason is written, specific, and
exactly the story.

**Band readings**

| | Material | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** ×2 | Sinew Cord (M-1) | A cord off a burning house. **Zero effect.** Deliberately worthless |
| **F2** ×4 | Sky-Iron (M-2) | A queen's private token. Opens doors that have guards on them |
| **F3** ×8 | **Orichalcum** (M-3) | The city-metal. **A writ. The capital attaches to the Lounge after F3, and this is what it attaches by** |

## C-7 — Hard × loot · **Crystal Shard**

|||
|---|---|

| **quant/cat** | **categorical** |
| **requirement_shape** | 🎯 **none** — loot |
| **cost** | 🔴 **cost-at-use, and it turns on the bearer** — the Lúin of Celtchar shape (W-4b) |

The plague, carried on purpose. Canon says the Crystal Spore Mist **tempts** — *no roll, it just
looks like a find, and every crystallized citizen walked toward it.* A shard in a pouch is that
temptation with a price tag: inhalation is Infected **plus Suffocation**, and the shard does not
distinguish between the party and the room.

⚠️ **The one item in Set 1 whose danger is the gate.** Myth prices raw effect with danger —
Lúin kills one per thrust, nine if cast, and *"if the lance is not quenched quickly… it will
pierce him."* No predicate needed; the thing is simply loaded.

## C-8 — Hard × crafted · **Kin-Carve** ⭐ the W-8 §3 exemplar

|||
|---|---|
| **essence** | 🔴 **bypass** — dragon-blood cuts what the plague has warded |
| **quant/cat** | **categorical** |
| **requirement_shape** | 🔒 **`REFUSES Animal Planet`** |
| **cost** | 🔴 **overuse makes you kin** — and every demon wants what you are becoming |

**Teigu's rule, and GPT is built for it:** *"Certain Teigu begin to fuse with the user if it is
overused, slowly transforming the user into the original Danger Beasts."* We already carve every
elite and boss; §12.7 already says **the striking part sets the band**; damage already lives in
**individual body parts**, so a partial transformation has somewhere to be recorded.

🔴 **And the canon makes it worse in the best way.** Loong blood cures demonic tendencies and
*every demon wants it.* A contestant slowly becoming Loong-like is **not carrying loot — they
are becoming livestock**, which is precisely what the hunt intends. The F2 and F3 hunts turn
onto them personally.

🔒 **`REFUSES Animal Planet`** — *"Establish a non-hostile relationship with a creature that has
no reason to tolerate you."* 🔴 **You cannot befriend the Loong and swing its kin.** A real fork
with no way to hold both sides, decidable on the sheet, and it is the Hard route's actual
question stated as a requirement.

⚠️ Distinct from the shipped **Loong-Scale Aegis** (armor, batch-c) and from M-5's apex
**Loong-Scale** (*canon — shed, not taken*). This is a **weapon**, carved from **Loong Kin**,
and the difference between *shed* and *taken* is the whole moral load.

**Band readings**

| | Material | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** ×2 | Beastbone (M-1) | Carved off the Warden Form. **Fusion has not started, and nobody warned you** |
| **F2** ×4 | Sky-Iron (M-2) | The Loong is *hurt and hunted*. Carrying its kin's bone through a demon hunt is a signal flare |
| **F3** ×8 | **Jade** (M-3) | The imperial stone. **The capital hunts the Loong for its blood — and you are walking in wearing some** |

## C-9 — Hard × story · **The Horn, taken**

|||
|---|---|
| **essence** | **dominion** — it commands the hunt rather than fighting it |
| **quant/cat** | **categorical** |
| **requirement_shape** | 🔒 **`REQUIRES Pinky Promise`** |
| **cost** | it calls hunters **wherever the target is**, and it cannot be aimed at nobody |

At F2 Hard the Horn (12 HP) *"sounds every Clock and a fresh pack arrives wherever the Loong
is."* Canon already lets the party **destroy** it to end the hunt. 🎯 **This concept adds the
second option: take it intact.**

⭐ **That is Elden Ring's Remembrance shape (W-7 §4), and the Lootbox already implements it** —
sealed contents, a reveal, `pick-one via /claim`, and a permanent Box Log rendering the unchosen
option **struck through.** One object, two outcomes, *"only one reward may be selected"*:

| Choice | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Destroy it** | The hunt ends. Clean, final, and you hold nothing |
| **Take it** | The hunt ends **and you hold the thing that calls it.** You now own a weapon whose only function is to point a pack at something |

🔒 **`REQUIRES Pinky Promise`** — *"Make a commitment out loud — to an ally, an enemy, or the
camera — and then watch it immediately complicate things."* 🔴 **Sounding a horn is a public
commitment by definition.** You cannot quietly own this.

**Band readings**

| | Material | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** ×2 | — | **Does not exist yet.** The hunt has not started; C-9 is the one concept with no F1 reading |
| **F2** ×4 | Flint (M-2) | Taken instead of broken. The village lives, and you are holding the reason it was in danger |
| **F3** ×8 | **Mirror-Bronze** (M-3) | Reflection. **Sound it in the capital and the hunt turns around** — the city's own hunters, pointed by its own instrument |

⚠️ **C-9 is deliberately the thinnest at F1.** W-6 §5: myth's story class *"only exists after a
problem is solved"* — an empty F1 cell is the acquisition class working correctly, not a gap.

## C-11 — What is NOT here

- **No stat blocks.** Damage, Moment cost and requirements come after the shapes are blessed.
  W-6 §2 constrains them already: **88% of myth weapons are categorical and not one is a flat
  stat-bonus item**, so none of these nine should read as `+X damage`.
- **No apex additions.** W-7 §1's ~3% ratio says the apex shelf stays roughly where it is;
  batch-c already holds it.
- **Sets 2 and 3 (18 more concepts).** Set 2's floors are undesigned and M-4 is names-only, so
  authoring them now would be inventing the story to fit the items. **Set 1 is the whole
  deliverable until F4–F6 exist.**
