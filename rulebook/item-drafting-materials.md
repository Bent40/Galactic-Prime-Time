# Item Drafting — The Materials Catalog (PROPOSAL)

**Date:** 2026-08-04 · **Status:** 🟡 PROPOSED — scavenged from stories per
ID-0.27d (primary source: the game repo's `docs/research/mythology/` library,
155 grounded entries swept across 14 traditions), tiered into floor bands, with
the damage numbers derived from the tiering. ⚖ everywhere. Governing system:
passover ID-8; rulings ID-0.26–28.

**The ladder (⚖ confirm):** each band step **doubles** damage/resist numbers.
×1 → ×2 → ×4 → ×8 → ×16 → ×32. Worked example: a Greatsword (3 Bleed/Crush
baseline) in Jade (F3, ×8) hits for 24 — right-sized for capital-era elites; an
apex-material build reaches 96 a swing, which is how a party takes apart a
thousands-HP monster without the sheet changing shape.

**Band availability tracks floors** but isn't locked to them — earlier bands
stay craftable forever (that's what commodity gear is), deeper bands only enter
the world when their floor does.

**The striking-part rule:** the blade/head material sets the damage band; every
socketed material contributes its inherent effect; affix slots (tier) are a
separate layer on top.

## M-7 — Guns, cannons & tech: "the part that touches the target carries the band"

- **Firearms, cannons, bows, crossbows: the AMMO is the blade.** Rounds,
  shells, bolts, and arrows are consumables **made from materials**
  (ID-0.27a): Beastbone shot, Sky-Iron slugs, Jade-tipped bolts. The
  projectile's material sets the damage band.
- **The delivery part caps the band ⚖:** a barrel (or bow limbs) fires ammo of
  its own band or below; firing above it bursts or degrades the weapon (GM).
  Upgrade the barrel to unlock deeper ammo — platform and payload are two
  separate chases.
- **Tech weapons (Fabricator exotics):** no bullets — the **emitter/core
  material** sets the band (§20.3's "exotic prints consume rare materials,"
  cashed in). Charges refill per §12.5.
- **The economy consequence:** ranged damage burns materials per shot; melee
  doesn't. That's the running cost that balances §12.1's per-round advantage.
  Fabricator prints Standard ammo free (canon); banded ammo consumes its
  material — ⚖ one material unit = one full magazine.

## M-8 — Carve, gather, reforge ⚖

- **Carve:** post-combat, out of danger — automatic, no roll ⚖; mid-combat
  carving is a 1-Moment action for the desperate/greedy (the camera zooms in).
  A boss yields its named material (the Incineradile: **mycelium-threaded
  hide** ⚖) + d3 band-material units. Mob-tier yields band scraps.
- **Claims:** carve yields go to a party material pool by default; the carver
  calls dibs on named boss materials ⚖ (table drama is a feature).
- **Gather:** floors offer their band's plant/stone materials as scenery
  interactions (GM-placed, Goals can spotlight them).
- **Reforge (RULED ID-0.28):** the Forge swaps any part's material; name,
  affixes, history survive. Cost ⚖: the new material + 1 UT per band step.
  Consumables excluded (ID-0.27a).
