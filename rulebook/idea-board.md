# Idea Board — campaign ideas, parked and unruled

A place to throw an idea so it is not lost. **Nothing here is ruled, statted or seeded.**
An entry is a note, not a design; when one is picked up it moves to the document that
owns it (an item concept to `set1-item-concepts.md`, a growth item to
`growth-items-and-parasites.md`, a rule to the rulebook) and the row here is struck.

Format: date · who · the idea as said · where it would plug in · open questions.

---

## IB-1 — The sword that grows with care (owner, 2026-10-07)

> *"a tiny sword that gets +1 range every time you sharpen it / work on it for a full
> downtime, or maybe x amount of time in the downtime, permanently."*

**The idea:** a small blade whose **range grows by one space per downtime of work**, and the
growth is **permanent**. The sword a contestant has been maintaining for ten floors is a very
different object from the one they picked up.

**Where it plugs in (nothing new needed to try it):**
- **§20.1 Downtime** already prices the cost: 2 downtime actions per contestant, one action =
  one module engagement. "A full downtime" is *both actions*; "x amount of time" is *one
  action*. Either reading is a number the book already has.
- **§12.1 range** is already a stat an item carries, and ranges may be stat-valued
  (*"Range: Reflexes"*), so a range that is a growing number is inside the existing shape.
- **The Growth subtype** (`ITEM_SUBTYPES`, `growth-items-and-parasites.md` G-0) is exactly
  this convention: a card with a public read and the mechanics held by the GM. Written as a
  Growth item, the player never reads *"+1 range per downtime"* — they notice, somewhere
  around the third floor, that the knife reaches further than it used to.

**Open (unruled):**
- One action or two? Two makes it compete with every other Lounge use, which is the price
  that keeps it interesting.
- Does the growth ever stop, and does it ever go down (a floor spent not sharpening)?
- Where does it come from — the shop (1 UT, Crude, indistinguishable from the junk beside it)
  or a drop? The shop reading fits the Growth shelf's camouflage rule in `the-shop.md` SH-2.
