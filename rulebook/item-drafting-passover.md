## ID-0 — Today's rulings (owner, 2026-08-04) — RULED

1. **Affixes: Lesser AND Normal are both designed** — the live app catalog is the
   source of truth (12 Lesser, 15 Normal). Book §12.3's "only Lesser is designed so
   far" is stale → fix in the book pass (ID-6).
2. **Mythic artifacts are plural.** More than one meta-breaking artifact exists in
   the world; a Mythic box yields exactly one (the pick-one-of-three is the
   *reveal*, not a scarcity rule).
3. **Armor = resistance.** Armor pieces grant flat resistance; **higher-tier gear
   can also grant nullification** (tier-immunity in §10 vocabulary). Numbers ⚖ — ID-2.
4. **Weapon-table "Cost" = Moments to use.** (Other cost axes possible later if we
   feel like it.)
5. **ALL items have tiers.** The Crude→Exceptional ladder applies to every
   category, and affixes can be authored for non-weapons (the catalog already has
   armor/shield/tool affixes: Barbed, Steady, Reactive).
6. **Exceptional is a polish tier, not a drop tier.** You *reach* it: via
   limited-chance upgrade items (Dark-Souls-slab-like) and/or **growth-type items**.
   Mechanic shapes proposed in ID-3 — direction ruled, numbers not.
7. **Limited-magic item = one specific magic.** An orb that casts fireball; the item
   is the spell's only source.
8. **Boxes are generic OR specific.** Specific = boss-specific, quest-specific, or
   floor-themed. Floor canon for theming: F1 green forest, F2 great desert (+70y),
   F3 grand capital (+100y); routes Easy (the mask/Nullrot), Medium (the demon
   girl/queen/farm), Hard (the Loong). F4–6 continent-merge — not yet designed, so
   their pools are out of scope for this pass.

## ID-1 — The authoring frame: subtypes and what each tier MEANS

**Subtype vocabulary (PROPOSED — canonize, it already exists implicitly in the affix
"Applicable to:" lines):**

> **Weapon:** Bladed · Crush · Martial (unarmed-class) · Ranged · Thrown
> **Wearable:** Armor (slot-anchored) · Shield · Trinket (ring-class)
> **Carried:** Tool · Consumable · Charged gear · Limited-magic · Upgrade/Growth (ID-3)
> **System:** Key Items · System Items (exempt from tiers/affixes)

Every authored item names its subtype so affix applicability, §12.1 class baselines,
and box pools can key off it. (App metadata question → ID-5.)

**Per-tier meaning by category (PROPOSED bands, ⚖ all numbers):**

| Tier | Weapons (§12.1/12.3 stand) | Armor/Shield | Tools | Consumables |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crude | class baseline or worse; 0/0 slots | 1 flat resist, one type, fragile (GM may break on a 1) | does the job with a Moment penalty or narrow scope | 1 use, weak effect |
| Basic | class baseline; 1/0, Lesser | 1 flat resist, one type | does the job | 1–2 uses, standard counter |
| Quality | baseline+ or a quirk; 1/1, ≤Normal | 2 flat resist OR 1 resist two types | scope widens; may remove a Forced-Action class | more uses or a rider |
| Superior | authored identity; 2/1, ≤Higher | 3 flat resist; may carry **T1 nullification** of its theme type | grants a capability (not just relief) | strong effect + rider |
| Exceptional | polished (ID-3); 2/2, ≤Legendary | 4 flat resist; **nullification** up to T2 / a full type ⚖ | near-skill-grade capability | (n/a — consumables cap at Superior ⚖) |

**Armor open sub-questions → ID-2.**

## ID-2 — Armor rules sliver (must rule before authoring armor)

- **A. Coverage:** armor protects the part(s) it occupies (torso plate → torso HP
  only)? PROPOSED: yes — slots follow anatomy (§12.4), so protection does too.
- **B. Stacking:** flat resists from different worn pieces stack when the same part
  is hit? PROPOSED: yes but only the struck part's armor counts; trinket resists
  (if any) are global ⚖.
- **C. Nullification:** which tiers, which shape? PROPOSED per the ID-1 table:
  Superior = T1 condition-nullification of the armor's theme type; Exceptional = T2,
  or full immunity to one flat type on the covered part ⚖.
- **D. Shields:** occupy a hand, grant resist without covering a specific part
  (block = the §14 dodge/cover layer handles the rest)? PROPOSED: shields give their
  resist to whatever part the wielder is defending — GM adjudicated, keep it loose ⚖.

## ID-3 — The Exceptional path (direction RULED, mechanic PROPOSED)

Two shapes, both can coexist:

- **Polish Kits (the slab analogue):** consumable upgrade item, tier-bound
  (a kit targets exactly Superior→Exceptional; lower rungs use the Forge). Applied at
  the Forge as a downtime action. **Limited chance** ⚖: d6 — 5–6 success · 2–4
  nothing, kit consumed · 1 mishap (item drops a modifier? never destroyed? ⚖).
  Acquisition: Gold+ boxes, deep-floor finds, never sold.
- **Growth items:** authored specials that level with their wielder — kill-count /
  milestone / story-trigger thresholds walk them up the tiers (a Crude seed that ends
  Exceptional). Each is hand-authored with its growth track; the first candidate is
  already sitting in canon: an **Incineradile mycelium core** that grows with its
  carrier ⚖.

Both paths respect §12.3 access as the item climbs (a grown item re-checks its
modifier access at each tier).
