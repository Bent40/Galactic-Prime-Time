## C-5 — The "Fireball?" chain (skill-side work item) ⚖

The Tome of the First Flame needs its chain authored as SkillTemplates. Sketch
for your shaping — every link is named with the question mark, priming the
players that the book isn't done with them:

> **Fireball?** (= Fire Ball exactly) → L5 reveals → **Firestorm?** (area,
> multi-Moment) → L5 → **Flame Wall?** (= Fire Wall+) → L5 → **Living Flame?**
> (companion ember, reactive) → L5 → **The First Flame.** (no question mark —
> the chain's end, god-tier fire, name and numbers owner-authored)

Chain skills follow normal skill rules (caps, Patron Tokens, priming) — the
tome breaks *acquisition*, not advancement. SkillTemplates to be authored via
the skill-library flow when the tome first lands in play.

## C-7 — Godly: authoring guidance (no pool, no seeds — by design)

Never random, never for sale, never generic. When a Godly box exists at all:
1. **Authored for the opener** — it answers who they ARE (the box knows). Draw
   from their verdict-axis record, their tags, their essence — not their build.
2. **Fate-defying, once** — it may break one thing even Narrative Tokens cannot
   ⚖, but never another player's agency, and it cannot mint more of itself.
3. **It arrives at an apex** — a Godly item is a story event with a body.
4. **The divinity end is populated** — patron gods, fallen-god game-runners
   (Cosmic Casino canon). A Godly item is *somebody's* attention made solid;
   author the somebody first, the stats last.
